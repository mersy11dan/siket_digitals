How Siket Digitals
looks, sounds, and behaves.

Brand rules for our own posts, documents, and staff. Not for client websites.

Version 1 · October 2026
Owner: the three founders. Changes need all three.
1 · The brand in one page

Clear, modern, and from Addis Ababa.

Everything we put out should feel like a well-set newspaper page: calm paper, dark ink, one warm accent. If a post looks loud, crowded, or like a tech startup template, it is not us.

Clear

Say one thing per post. Short sentences. No jargon the client would not use.

Modern

Current tools, current design. We show the work, not stock photos of laptops.

Local

We are a team in Addis Ababa building for businesses here first. Say it with pride, not as a disclaimer.

The line we use

Modern websites for growing businesses. This is the tagline. Use it on covers, bios, and the end of decks. Do not rewrite it per post.

The name

  • Always Siket Digitals. Two words, capital S and D, with the s on Digitals.
  • Never "Siket Digital", "SiketDigitals", "SIKET", or "Siket" alone in writing. In speech, "Siket" is fine.
  • In Amharic text, keep the name in Latin letters: Siket Digitals.
2 · Logo

One mark. Two correct placements.

The mark is the green S with the orange arrow. Use the official PNG or SVG from the shared folder. Do not redraw it, trace it, or export it from a screenshot.

Siket Digitals

On ink. Mark plus the name in the serif. The e is orange. This is the main lockup for covers, profile banners, and the end slide.

On paper. Mark alone, when the name is already on the page or in the handle. Use this for post corners and profile pictures.

Clear space and size

Keep space around the mark equal to the height of the arrow tip (x). Nothing crosses that line: no text, no photo edge, no other logo.

  • Smallest size on screen: 32 px tall. In print: 10 mm.
  • Profile picture: mark alone, centred on paper or on ink, with the clear space inside the circle.
  • In post corners the mark is small (about 1/14 of the post width) and sits bottom right.
  • On a photo, put the mark in the darkest or quietest corner, never over a face.

Do and do not

Do

  • Use the official file at its original proportions.
  • Place it on paper, ink, or a calm photo area.
  • Keep green for the S and orange for the arrow.
  • Set the name in Newsreader (or Georgia) beside it.

Do not

  • Stretch, rotate, outline, add a shadow, or add a glow.
  • Recolor it, make it white, or make it one color.
  • Put it on a busy pattern, a gradient, or another brand's color.
  • Write the name in another typeface or in all caps.

4 · Typography

A serif for the headline, a plain sans for everything else.

Headlines are set in Newsreader. Body, labels, and captions are set in Outfit. Both are free on Google Fonts and are available inside Canva. If you cannot use them, use Georgia and Segoe UI (or Calibri) instead. Never use Arial Black, Montserrat, Poppins, or script fonts.

Rules

  • Align left. Centre text only on a cover or a single-line quote.
  • Headlines in sentence case, with a full stop. Never all caps.
  • All caps is only for kickers: the small label above a headline, letter-spaced.
  • One typeface change per layout: serif headline, sans body. No third font.
  • Line length 45 to 70 characters. If the text is wider, make the box narrower.

The highlighted word

  • One word in a headline may be orange, when it is the point: "Launched this week."
  • Only one word, only in a headline, only once per post.
  • The e in Siket in the wordmark is orange. Nothing else in the name is.

A post has a kicker at the top, one headline in the middle, and a thin footer with the handle on the left and the mark on the right. That is the whole system. Photos, screenshots, and mockups go behind it or beside it.

Photos and screenshots

Do

  • Use our own photos and real screenshots of our work.
  • Natural light, simple backgrounds, people doing the work.
  • Show websites inside a plain phone or laptop frame, on paper or night.
  • Crop tight. One subject per image.

Do not

  • No stock photos of handshakes, laptops with coffee, or glowing code.
  • No heavy filters, no AI-generated people, no neon gradients.
  • No screenshots with the client's private data, admin pages, or unfinished copy.
  • No memes on the company account. Personal accounts are yours.

Captions

  • First line is the point, in one sentence. The rest can be skipped and still make sense.
  • Three to six short lines. Then one call to action: "DM us" or "Link in bio" or the phone number.
  • Hashtags: three to five, at the end, on their own line. Always include #SiketDigitals and #AddisAbaba. Never more than eight.
  • Emoji: at most one, and only where a word would not do. Never in headlines on the image.
  • Amharic captions are welcome. Keep the brand name in Latin letters and keep the same tone.
6 · How we write

Plain, confident, and specific.

We talk like a capable person explaining something to a friend who runs a shop. We do not talk like a brochure.

Sound like this

"We rebuilt the menu page so it loads in under two seconds on a cheap phone. Orders went up the first week."

Not like this

"We leverage cutting-edge solutions to deliver seamless, world-class digital experiences that drive synergy."

RuleDetail
Short sentencesIf a sentence needs a comma and an "and" and a "which", make it two.
Say what, not how great"Loads in two seconds" beats "lightning fast". Numbers beat adjectives.
Words we usewebsite, web app, redesign, launch, build, client, business, works on a phone, get in touch.
Words we avoidsolutions, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, synergy, world-class, 10x, unlock, revolutionary, "digital transformation".
PunctuationFull stops at the end of headlines. No exclamation marks on the company account. No em-dashes; use a comma, a full stop, or a colon.
We, not IThe company account speaks as "we". Name the person when it is their work: "Ribka designed it."
PricesNever post prices publicly. Say "Message us and we will quote within a day."
ClientsName a client only after launch and only with their yes. Before that: "a clinic in Bole", "a logistics company".
7 · Staff

Each of us is the brand when a client is looking.

These apply to the three founders and to anyone who joins or freelances with us. They are about how we show up, not about controlling your personal life.

Profiles

  • LinkedIn headline: your role, then "at Siket Digitals". Example: "Backend developer at Siket Digitals".
  • Profile photo: a recent, front-facing photo, plain background, natural light, shoulders up. Same photo on LinkedIn, the website, and the decks, updated together.
  • Link Siket Digitals as your current company so it appears on the company page.
  • Personal accounts are yours. If you mention client work there, follow the same client rules as the company account.

Replying to clients

ChannelRule
EmailReply within one working day, even if only to say when the full answer is coming. Subject lines say the project and the topic: "Clinic site: homepage copy".
WhatsApp and TelegramReply within a few hours during working hours (9:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday). Outside those hours, it can wait, unless a live site is down.
CallsPick up when you can; if you cannot, send a message with a time you will call back.
ToneWarm, brief, and clear. Greet by name. One question per message when you need a decision. Confirm agreements in writing after a call.
Bad newsSay it early, say what you will do about it, and give the new date. Never go quiet.

Meetings

  • On time means five minutes early. If you will be late, message before the start time.
  • Dress clean and simple. No slogans, no other company's branding. Smart casual is right for almost every client.
  • Bring the laptop charged with the work ready to show, and a notebook. Phones face down.
  • End every meeting with: what we decided, who does what, and by when. Send that summary the same day.

What stays private

  • Client data, logins, API keys, and the contents of any .env file never leave the project. Never paste them in chats, screenshots, or posts.
  • Client prices, contracts, and unlaunched work are confidential until the client says otherwise.
  • Do not discuss one client's project with another client.
  • Code is in the company GitHub. Personal copies are for working, not for sharing.

8 · Documents

Decks, proposals, and invoices look like the website.

Decks

16:9, paper pages with a colored stripe on the left, night cover and end slide. Mark on the cover, page number bottom right, "Siket Digitals · Addis Ababa" bottom left. Start from the Company Profile deck.

Proposals and quotes

A4, paper or white for printing. Mark top left, client name and date top right. Sections: what we understood, what we will build, what it costs, what happens next, and how long it takes.

Invoices and receipts

A4. Mark top left, "Invoice" in the serif, a plain table, bank details in a Field box, the studio email and phone in the footer. Numbered SD-2026-001 and up.

File names

  • Pattern: Siket Digitals - Client - Document - YYYY-MM-DD. Example: Siket Digitals - Bole Clinic - Proposal - 2026-10-03.pdf.
  • Send PDFs, not editable files, unless the client asks.
  • One shared folder per client. Final files in a Sent subfolder.
9 · Before you post or send

Thirty seconds that save a correction.

  • Is the name written Siket Digitals?
  • Is there one headline, one point, one accent color?
  • Is the mark the official file, in the corner, with clear space?
  • Serif headline, sans body, no third font?
  • Paper or night background, not white, not a gradient?
  • Any client named only after launch and with their yes?
  • No prices, no private data, no admin screens?
  • No exclamation marks, no em-dashes, no jargon from the avoid list?
  • Three to five hashtags, including #SiketDigitals?
  • Did one other founder read it?
